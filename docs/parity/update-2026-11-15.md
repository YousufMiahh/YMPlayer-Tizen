## Left out, and why

- **Phone only**: Downloads (and "Download over Wi-Fi only"), Cast, Picture in Picture, pinch to zoom,
  touch gestures, the bottom bar, the mini player. A TV app has no use for them.
- **Still not possible in a Tizen web app** (unchanged from the last report): the in-app updater and
  Check for Updates, VLC / engine choice, Audio Sync, phone sign-in.

## To check on the TV

- **Fill screen (zoom)** asks AVPlay for a picture box larger than the screen. If the TV refuses
  that, the app falls back to filling the screen the plain way (Stretch). Fit, Stretch, 16:9 and
  4:3 use ordinary boxes.
- **Skip Intro** and **Because You Watched** call `api.themoviedb.org` and `api.theintrodb.org`
  from the app (allowed by `<access origin="*">` in `config.xml`).
- The TMDB key is the one already published in the Android app's source.
